    Privacy & legal

    What this game stores

    Kaido Drift sets no cookies. It keeps one entry in your browser's local storage on this device: a random player ID, your driver name, credits, owned cars, paint choices and settings. That entry is strictly necessary for the game to remember your progress and never leaves your device. There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

    The only network requests are for the game's code library (three.js from the jsDelivr CDN) and its fonts (Google Fonts). Those providers can see your IP address as part of any normal web request.

    You can erase everything at any time under Settings → Erase progress, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

    AI drivers

    The other drivers and every chat message they send are computer-controlled and marked AI. Nothing you type in chat is sent anywhere; it stays on your device.
